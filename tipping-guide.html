The bill just came. Now what?

Here's exactly what to say, and how much to actually tip in Morocco — restaurants, cafes, taxis, and riads.

🍽️ Asking for the bill

L-hsab, afak
(l-HSAB, a-FAK) — "The bill, please"

Say this instead of waving or making a scribbling motion — waiters respond faster to the phrase than to gestures, and it signals you're not a first-timer.

💰 How much to actually tip

Restaurants (no service charge)10%
Restaurants (service charge included)Round up / 5%
Cafes (coffee, tea)3–5 MAD
TaxisRound up to nearest 5–10 MAD
Riad staff (per stay)50–100 MAD
Tour guides (per day)100–150 MAD
The trap most tourists fall into: in tourist-heavy areas, some restaurants quietly add a service charge to the bill and then still expect a tip on top — always check the bill line by line before deciding how much to leave.

🙅 If something looks wrong on the bill

Ma fhemtsh had ttaman
(ma-FHEMTSH had ta-MAN) — "I don't understand this price"

Calm, not confrontational — this phrase alone gets most billing mistakes corrected on the spot.
